PulseDrop

PulseDrop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

PulseDrop is a portrait arcade game. Campaign and Endless can be played offline. The game has no separate PulseDrop account or game servers. Optional gameplay analytics are off unless you enable them in the welcome tutorial or Settings → Privacy. When Apple Game Center is available and you are signed in, PulseDrop authenticates through Game Center and submits completed-run scores to Apple's leaderboards. PulseDrop offers optional rewarded videos served by Google AdMob; they play only when you tap the watch button.

Information stored on your device

PulseDrop stores campaign progress, scores, ship unlocks, run history, and settings locally in the app's storage. Local progress is not synchronized between devices. If you are signed in to Game Center, completed-run scores are also sent to Apple for its leaderboards; Apple handles Game Center account and leaderboard data under Apple's privacy practices. Sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings to stop score submissions. Removing PulseDrop from your device removes its local save.

Audio and permissions

PulseDrop plays music and sound effects bundled with the app. It does not record microphone audio. The game does not request location, contacts, photos, or camera access. PulseDrop does not track you across other companies' apps and websites and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Where the law requires it, a consent form from Google appears when you choose to watch a rewarded video.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics are off by default. If you choose Share gameplay data in the welcome tutorial or enable it in Settings → Privacy, PulseDrop sends a summary to TelemetryDeck when a run that began with sharing enabled ends. The summary contains the ship, game mode, stage reached, number of stages cleared, outcome, and time spent actively playing. It does not contain your name, Game Center account, or advertising identifier. PulseDrop generates a random identifier for this app installation and hashes it before sending it so run summaries can be counted; TelemetryDeck receives the IP address needed to deliver the request and describes its handling of this information in its privacy documentation.

If you play offline, up to 100 unsent summaries are kept on your device and retried when you return to the app or finish another run. Turning sharing off stops future uploads, discards unsent summaries, and removes the local identifier. Information already received by TelemetryDeck is retained under its policies. A run closed before it ends may not produce a summary.

Rewarded videos and advertising

PulseDrop offers optional rewarded videos in exchange for in-game Credits. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Nothing is requested from AdMob until you tap the watch button. The game is fully playable without watching any video.

When you choose to watch a video, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data from your device, such as your IP address, device and app identifiers, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This data is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. PulseDrop does not receive this data and does not combine it with any other information.

More specifically, when a rewarded video is requested, Google may collect: device identifiers; approximate location derived from your IP address; advertising data and ad and product interaction data (such as which ads were shown and how they were used); and diagnostic data (crash data, performance data and other diagnostics). Google may link this data to your device. It is used for third-party advertising, for advertising by the app developer, for analytics, and for app functionality such as consent handling, fraud prevention and ad delivery. PulseDrop does not itself link this data to you, and does not use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites as Apple defines tracking: the App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown, so the advertising identifier is not available to the app.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), PulseDrop asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting an ad. PulseDrop requests only non-personalized ads and does not use the advertising identifier. You can decline the consent form, and the game remains fully playable. You can change your consent at any time using Ad privacy choices in Settings → Privacy (shown where consent rules apply).

Purchases

PulseDrop offers an optional in-app purchase of Credits. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; PulseDrop does not receive or store your payment details or Apple Account information. PulseDrop saves only the fact that a purchase's Credits were granted (an App Store transaction number) in its local save, so the same purchase is never credited twice.

Sharing and sale of information

Game Center is an Apple service and uses the Apple account already configured on the device; PulseDrop does not receive the player's Apple account credentials or email address. AdMob receives information for rewarded videos as described above, and TelemetryDeck receives gameplay summaries only if you enable sharing. PulseDrop does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Saved local progress remains on your device until you remove the app. PulseDrop does not keep a remote copy. Scores submitted to Game Center are managed through Apple's Game Center service and account settings. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies; use the controls above to limit it.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if PulseDrop's data practices change, and the effective date above will be revised.
