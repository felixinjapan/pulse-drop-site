Questions, bug reports, or feedback: staryul@naver.com

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Is my progress saved between devices?

No. Campaign progress, scores, ship unlocks, run history, and settings are stored only on the device you play on, and are not synced. Deleting the app removes the local save.

How do I stop scores going to Game Center?

Scores are submitted only while you are signed in to Game Center. Sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings to stop submissions. Game Center data is managed by Apple.

How do I delete my data?

PulseDrop keeps no account and no server-side copy. Removing the app from your device deletes its local data. Scores already sent to Game Center are managed through your Apple account settings.

The game feels too bright or busy

Open Settings (top of the main screen) and turn on Reduce visual effects. This reduces flashes, particles, and screen shake. The system Reduce Motion setting is also respected.
